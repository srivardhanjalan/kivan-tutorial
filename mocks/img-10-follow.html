One edge, two counts

A tap writes a single row, but only if it is new, then nudges the two denormalized tallies the profile reads in one shot.

A repeat follow fails the condition and touches no count, so the tallies move exactly once per real change. FollowingIndex flips the edge so "who follows them" is a query too. The counts are a cache; the edge is the truth.
